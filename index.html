FAQ

What’s the age policy?

18+ after 21:00. Under-16s are welcome on Saturday and Sunday afternoons with an adult.

Can I bring a bag?

Small bags only, around A4 size. No glass, no gazebos and no air horns. Especially no air horns.

How do I get there?

Shuttle ferries leave the city pier every 20 minutes until 03:30. There’s secure bike parking at the east gate and no car parking at all.

Is the site accessible?

Every stage has a raised viewing platform, step-free routes are marked on the map, and our access team answers emails within a day.

What does “no phones on the dancefloor” mean?

We put a sticker over your camera at the gate. Take photos anywhere else; the floor in front of the speakers is for dancing.

Is this festival real?

Not yet. STATIC//BLOOM is a concept site made for a design portfolio, so nothing is sold here.
